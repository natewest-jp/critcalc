# src/lib — schema, data conventions and Rotation Planner internals

Moved verbatim out of the root `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-09-24 so it loads only when a session works under `src/lib/`. The root file's rules still apply here.

### Key Types

- **`FrameMap` is gcsim's slice shape (gauge round L4b, 2026-08-16)** — `{ anim, hit?, …overrides }`. ⚓⚓ **`anim` is required and IS the fallback, not `swap`**: gcsim fills every slot with the animation length and then applies overrides, of which `ActionSwap` is merely one and frequently the shortest. Measured, a charged attack after a normal was timed **22 frames early in 266 of 297 maps**. `hit` is `InitNormalCancelSlice`'s hitmark and fills E/Q/D/J/swap, so a normal swing is two numbers (`{anim:35, hit:24}`); ⛔ it is a lint error on an E/Q slice, which has no such tier. **`frameAt()` in `$lib/utils/frames.ts` is the ONLY resolver** — ⛔ never re-inline `fm[k] ?? fm.swap`. ⚓ **Plunges are two fields**: `plungeFrames` is the HIGH plunge and the default (`P`/`hP`), `lowPlungeFrames` is `lP` — ⚠️⚠️ the field CHANGED MEANING at L4b, since 36 of its 41 authors held LOW-plunge numbers. ⛔ gcsim's `ActionCharge = 500` is a "this action is illegal" SENTINEL, not data, and is dropped
- **`application[]`** (`ElementalApplication`, L4c) — **when a cast's own hits LAND and what they apply**, the thing the corpus could never say. ⚓ **An EMPTY array is the positive statement that a cast applies nothing** (39 abilities: Xiangling's E, Xiao's Q, every stance opener); an ABSENT field means unmeasured and falls back to applying the character's element at the cast, counted provisional. ⚠️ It is the NOMINAL schedule (contract C4) — gcsim emits a damage event for a hit its own ICD suppressed — and ⛔ normals carry no list (their hitmark is `frames.hit`, their element has one owner in `resolveActionElement`) nor do summons (`interval`/`firstTickDelay` already say it). ⚓ A cast's own hits share ONE ICD counter; ⛔ counters are NOT shared across casts — 112 of 166 gcsim skills are `ICDTagNone` and Xiao's tagged skill resets in 5 frames. Read `docs/gauge-round-L4.md`
- **`Ability`** / **`AbilityVariant`** — flat structure, `FrameMap` timing per hit; `ConstellationAbility.constellationLevel` is the canonical gate. ⚠️ A variant's `effects` and `summons` **REPLACE** the base ability's, never merge — so a variant restating one effect drops the rest — and an empty `summons: []` means **NONE**, not "inherit the base". The parser did not read variant `effects` at all until 2026-08-14 (`7d5aabc`), which had five characters' hold-E rendering as their tap
- **`Effect`** — discriminated union (buff/damage/heal/shield/resShred/energy/infuse/…); heal/damage/**shield** carry optional `flat`; **shield** also carries `label` (its in-game name, rendered by the shield lane — ⛔ never invent one); `Condition` trees gate effects (strict `condition-shape` lint)
- **`AbilityTag`** — `heal | shield | summon | taunt | coordinatedAttack | cleanse | grouping` (lint-enforced vocabulary)
- **`summonTick.tickCap` / `tickCapScaling` (2026-08-18)** — how many ticks a series fires, when the count is not `duration ÷ interval`. ⚓ Absent is right for **26 of the 37** series the oracle can compare, so ⛔ it is not a field to fill in by default. ⚓⚓ **A COUNT, never a time bound** — Mona's gcsim loop caps at `i < 300` FRAMES while her summon's measured lifetime is ~4.05s, so the two genuinely differ and ⛔ nudging a stated `duration` is the fix her own notes refuse. The rule is `tickCapOf` in `sim/summons.ts` and ⚠️ **BOTH readers must honour it** (`collectSummonTicks` → the markers a player counts, `aura.ts`'s chain → the gauge; `run.ts` forbids unifying them). ⚠️ A cap widens the window by exactly ONE interval so the BOUNDARY tick lands — our walk is half-open and gcsim's status-gated chains are not (Kaeya's `Status.Add(key, duration + hitmark + 1)`, where the `+1` is deliberate). ⛔ **A cap and a `durationExtensions` do not compose** — Collei is uncapped for that reason. ⚠️⚠️ **A sibling HEAL series on the same cadence must be capped too**: gcsim emits both from one task (Kuki's `bellTick`), and the oracle cannot see it because a heal leaves no `damage` event. Lint D.27d; 14 damage + 4 heal sites across 11 characters. Read `docs/gauge-round-L5e.md` §4
- **`BuffEffect.onFieldDelay` (2026-08-18)** — how long the RECEIVER must have been on field before a buff lands. Nicole's Methexis ("after the observed character has remained on the field for 3s") and her C4, which is granted BY that upgrade. ⚠️ Only meaningful on `target: 'active'` (lint B.14): a per-receiver slice is the one scope that knows who is receiving and when they arrived — the same structural claim `activeCharFaction` and `weaponType` make. ⚓⚓ **Its waiver is a `Condition`, not a bespoke key**, which is what keeps it one field: Nicole's two waivers already exist in that vocabulary and COMPOSE as one `any` — a Hexerei RECEIVER skips the wait (`activeCharFaction`; ⛔ never `teamFaction`, gcsim checks a bare `char.IsHexerei` with no count gate) and at C6 everyone does. ⚠️ Only a definitive TRUE waives. ⛔ **Don't reach for the two-effect pattern `activeCharFaction`'s own doc recommends when the pair shares a `stat`** — the active-target pass dedupes by stat BEFORE per-segment evaluation and silently keeps whichever was authored first. 🐛 It also exposed a live bug: the extension to a buff's end was keyed to the last SURVIVING SEGMENT rather than the last TRACK, so a dropped segment ran the previous receiver's buff through a swap-out that had already happened
- **`whileNormalAttacking` + a proc's `icdGate` (2026-08-18)** — the coordinated attack. ⚓ A `damage` effect carrying its own `application[]` on a trigger that listens to somebody ELSE's output is now the shape of FOUR things: `onReaction` (a reaction happened), `onHit` (the opponent took damage), `whileNormalAttacking` (the active character is swinging), all armed by a cast's `armedFor` or by a summon's own liveness, all dispatched by ONE registry (`sim/procs.ts`). ⚠️⚠️ **`icdGate` is NOT `procCooldown`**: the cooldown says how often a proc FIRES, the gate says how often a fire APPLIES — Raiden's Eye fires every 0.9s and applies every third, Thoma's collapse lands every second and applies every third. Absent = STANDARD, same as `summonTick.icdGate`, which is why the two shipped reaction procs (Nahida, Fischl A4) had to author `'none'` to keep what omission used to give them. ⚠️⚠️ **Pair the gate with the stream OUR effect models, never with the character** (the Emilie rule): a wave is ONE hit here and two-to-five swords in gcsim. ⚠️ `whileNormalAttacking` is summon-hosted ONLY (lint B.13c) and its markers come from `TimelineWindow.ticks`, ⛔ never from `collectSummonTicks`. Read `docs/coordinated-attacks-round.md`
- **A TICK SERIES CAN CHANGE CADENCE (2026-08-18)** — authored as the SAME series TWICE with mutually exclusive `conditions`, not as an interval override. ⚓ Two members, and the class is closed (swept: gcsim's other `tickrate`/`Interval` users are internal 0.1–0.3s samplers, and Nahida's is a `procCooldown`, already out of scope): **Aino's burst** goes 1.5s → 0.7s under Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam (`teamFaction: moonsign >= 2`) and **Ororon's** 1s → 0.8s at C4. ⚓⚓ **Two series because the rate is not the only thing that moves** — Aino's ICD GROUP changes with it (`ICDGroupDefault` 150f/every-3 → `ICDGroupAinoBurstMoonHit` 108f/every-4) and her count goes 10 → 21, so her applications go from every 3s to every 2.1s; ⚠️ Ororon's group does NOT travel with his rate, which is exactly why his note authored the group rather than the coincidence that matched it. ⚠️⚠️ **`summonTickSeries` drops only a DEFINITIVE false**, so a gated pair whose condition is UNRESOLVABLE does not abstain — it fires BOTH: the live chain had no condition context and Aino drew 10 markers while applying 12. ⛔ Every reader of a tick series must take the same `ConditionContext`. ⚠️ Ororon's 0.8s is a deliberate 0.05s divergence from gcsim's hardcoded 0.75 — "rotates 25% faster" is a RATE — and ⚠️⚠️ **the oracle can adjudicate neither character**: the dumper runs one character at C0 in an empty core, so it never reaches either branch
- **`gainStacks.scope` (2026-08-31)** — `'segment'` (default, every corpus key but one) or `'rotation'`. ⚓⚓ **A stack key names a RESOURCE, and a resource either survives a swap or it does not** — Alyosha's Hunter's Mark is a state on the OPPONENT, so `N4 > Fischl E > E` must still find it. A rotation-scoped key lives in `CharState.rotationStacks`/`rotationStackInstances` and is MIRRORED into the live segment pool at every settle point, so ⛔ not one existing reader changed: the scope decides the CONTAINER, never the reader. ⚠️⚠️ **It is a different axis from `trigger.source`** — that says who may FIRE the effect (`'team'`/`'others'` grants already accrue rotation-wide because the HOLDER may be off field), this says where the stacks LIVE; Hunter's Mark is `source: 'self'` AND rotation-scoped, and lint refuses `scope` on a team/others grant rather than give one key two homes. ⚓⚓ **It is also the one scope a SUMMON TICK can write**: the tick dispatchers are handed a **NULL** segment pool, so a segment-scoped grant is skipped BY CONSTRUCTION — ⛔ never hand them the on-field character's pool "so they work". ⚠️ Declare it on EVERY grant of the key (⛔ not the `drain` first-only convention): lint D.39 catches a disagreement, and an omission is invisible and would silently revert the key. Read `docs/summon-tick-stacks-round.md` §10
- **`onStack` — a STACK THRESHOLD (2026-09-18, `docs/stack-threshold-round.md` §5)** — `{ keys, count, dominant?, label? }` fires the instant the SUM of `keys` reaches `count` and ⚓⚓ SPENDS the set (implicitly — the only semantics under which it fires twice; a GATE is a `stacks` Condition, not this). `sim/thresholds.ts` is the ONE reader, at the four settle points where a count moves (reaction, cast, hit, tick), precomputed per team so it is free elsewhere. ⚓ A key is read from its ONE home, never summed across homes; a segment-home key is readable only where the on-field pool is in hand. A `damage` on it draws a MARK on the accruing summon's lane in the hit's element and ⛔ applies NOTHING (`application: []` required — Columbina's Interference is Durability 0); a `shield` opens at the fire under the host cast's source; a `buff` anchors on the `stackThreshold` event at the three NB-shaped sites with the `dominant` gate per event. ⚓ **`gainStacks.accrual {group, window}` is CONTINUOUS ACCRUAL** (`sim/accrual.ts`): `amount` becomes a RATE per second polled every 3 frames while a shared status is live, opened/refreshed by the grant's reaction and by a Lunar-Charged tick (the gauge's `ecTick`, fed to accrual grants ONLY); settled lazily and MONOTONE so a late-surfacing tick can refresh retroactively; the fire is predicted (`predictFire`) and scheduled, generation-stamped. `procCooldown` on an onReaction grant is honoured too (`onReactionProcLastFire`), but ⛔ it is not how a rate is stated. Lint B.16 / B.13k / D.40. Guarded by `sim/thresholds.test.ts`
- **`summonsFollowState` + `TransientStateSpec.toggle` (2026-09-04)** — the cast that opens ONE window whose OCCUPANT is re-chosen mid-flight. ⚓⚓ Furina's E summons the Salon Members (Ousia, the base ability) or the Singer (Pneuma, a state-gated VARIANT) on one shared 20s cooldown, and a Charged Attack flips the alignment: the running lineup ends at the flip and the other spawns there with the remaining duration ("the new guests will inherit the remaining duration" — the kit's own words; gcsim `charge.go:61`). ⛔⛔ **The flag is opt-in and the general rule would be WRONG** — a burst variant gated on `inStance` whose stance ends mid-window must KEEP the summon it spawned; a cast spawning a thing is the norm, a cast holding a slot is the exception. ⚓ **`toggle: true` is the second arm of `TransientStateSpec`** (no `duration` — a toggle has no length, only an other edge) and ⚓ **it names the NON-DEFAULT half**: the state is `pneuma`, its absence IS Ousia, so the default costs nothing. ⛔ Never mint an `ousia` beside it. ⚠️⚠️ It re-resolves at state edges the parse can SEE, ⛔ never at a timed window's silent expiry — **lint enforces that the gating state is a toggle**. 🆕 `actionOpensState.charged`; 🆕 `CastEvent.variantLabel`, a **THREE-STATE** (`string` = that variant · `null` = the base · `undefined` = match by input) because conflating "resolved no variant" with "caller has no cast in hand" is the exact defect — measured, every Ousia cast drew the Singer. ⚠️ A `summonsFollowState` window cannot be reconstructed from bar geometry, so `notationParser.ir.test.ts` asserts its occupants **TILE** it instead of naming them. - **`terminatesOnAction` + `onEnd` on a `TransientStateSpec`, and `NormalAbility.nonHitting` (2026-09-16, the Lauma charged-forms round — `docs/lauma-charged-forms-round.md`, §9 is the record)** — two primitives with one holder. ⚓ **A state can end on the owner's next ATTACK or CAST** (N, C, P, E, Q — ⚓ Nate: ⛔ never a dash or a jump, Lauma moves in deer form) **and CHAIN into another when it ends** (`onEnd: {state, duration}`, by ANY cause: expiry, action, swap, `consumes`). "A cooldown on something other than an E/Q" is, in this vocabulary, a window that opens when another closes — a NAMED state, never a `cooldown` number, because the thing that consumes it is a sibling ability's `conditions` and a condition needs a name to read. ⚠️⚠️ **`settleOwnerStates` runs at the top of every attack and cast branch BEFORE the state snapshot** — that ordering is the whole `C C` case (the second C ends the deer, opens the cooldown, resolves against it as Prayer); ⛔ `computeCurrentStates` stays a pure reader. ⚠️ A silent expiry fires nothing, so an expired window's chain is materialised LAZILY (next settle point, or finalize if its start is inside the rotation) with the start it always had; ### Data Conventions

- **Values at TALENT LEVEL 10** (sidecar rows' 10th column) — enforced by the normalize script
- `atk%` buff values are **whole numbers** (store `22.5` not `0.225`); `BuffValue` scaling ratios likewise percentage points
- **Never fake timing** — tick intervals/durations only from gcsim/kit text; `~?s` for unknowns; `_curatorNotes` for unmodelable effects
- **`caveat` strings follow `docs/curation/caveat-style-guide.md`** (player-facing voice, ≤2 sentences, no curator-speak; lint-enforced by characterLint E.26). ⚓⚓ **The test is NAME THE PIXEL (2026-08-19)** — a caveat exists only to repair confusion ON SCREEN, so if you cannot point at a bar, marker, tick or printed value that is wrong, approximate or conspicuously missing, it does not exist; ⛔ a general fact about the GAME is never a caveat, ⚠️ an under-claim is an omission not a divergence, and ⚠️ "no window" means "no row" means NO BLANK. The corpus went **261 → 107** on that rule. ⛔⛔ **A caveat may only live on an ABILITY-shaped host** (ability/passive/constellation ENTRY) — **lint E.28**, strict: 20 effect-level caveats rendered to NOBODY, and ⚠️⚠️ no key-name check can catch the class because `caveat` is legal on `AbilityCommon` and illegal there, so the defect is POSITIONAL and `orphanedSchema.test.ts` stayed green over all 20. ⛔⛔ **`manual` triggers reach NO surface and never did** (one code occurrence — its own type declaration; 167 effects across 69 characters), so ⛔ never write "add it manually". ⚠️ The engine-capability rubric is a SNAPSHOT (`docs/curation/engine-capability-rubric.md`) — **re-derive it at the start of any caveat pass**; the previous edition lived inside the style guide and went stale in 14 places in four weeks. Read memory `caveat_refresh_round`
- `caveat` = user-facing (plain language); `_curatorNotes` = curator-only (technical)
- Frameless characters live in `FRAMELESS_EXEMPT` (characterLint.test.ts) and render honestly ("not yet curated" bucket in the Team Builder; generic cast-time fallbacks in the planner). **Frames flip `isCurated` — never land frames on an effect-empty kit**
- Terminology aligns with **KQM (Keqing Mains)** standards throughout
- ⚓ **A FACTION TIER IS A CONDITION ON THE EFFECT, never a runtime state the parser tracks** (Silken Moon's Serenade, 2026-08-15 — the reference to copy as faction sets multiply). Ascendant Gleam is `{ type: 'teamFaction', faction: 'moonsign', min: 2 }` (sanctioned in `docs/library/game/factions.md`; Aino already uses it in five places), the Nascent rung is the same gate wrapped in `not`, and `baseCtx` already carries `teamFactionCounts` into the artifact path — so tiering cost ZERO code. ⚠️ Check the tier-0 case: gcsim returns early on Moonsign level 0, so the set gives NOTHING with no Moonsign character, and the previous unconditional value was wrong in every non-Moonsign team. ⚠️ `twoPiece` is declared on `ArtifactSetBonus` and read by **zero** call sites — Instructor's +80 EM and VV's +15% Anemo DMG are inert data, so don't add a Stat key for a 2pc until a consumer exists

## Rotation Planner — Key Internals

- **`RotationTimeline.svelte`** — Gantt timeline: E/Q/active/buff rows, cast markers, swap connectors, zoom/scroll; CodeMirror notation editor (lazy-loaded — **never statically import notationEditor from planner-reachable code**)

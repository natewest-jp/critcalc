# What the schema's fields mean

Budget: about 12,000 characters. Each entry: what the field claims, the rule that keeps it honest, and where the reasoning lives. Authoring conventions are in `.claude/rules/data.md`.

## Timing and hits

- **`FrameMap`** is gcsim's slice shape, `{ anim, hit?, …overrides }`. `anim` is required and is the fallback for every slot (gcsim fills every slot with the animation length, then applies overrides; `swap` is just one override). `hit` is the hitmark tier filling E/Q/D/J/swap, so a normal swing is `{anim, hit}`; `hit` on an E/Q slice is a lint error. ⚠️ `frameAt()` in `$lib/utils/frames.ts` is the only resolver: never re-inline `fm[k] ?? fm.swap`. `lands` states when the hits land where `hit` doesn't (every charged attack and plunge, most catalyst normals); ⚠️ read it only through `landsAt()` and `landingsOf()` in the same file. `plungeFrames` is the high plunge and the default (`P`/`hP`); `lowPlungeFrames` is `lP`. gcsim's `ActionCharge = 500` is an "illegal action" sentinel and is dropped. Record: `docs/rounds/gauge-round-L4.md`.
- **`application[]`** says when a cast's own hits land and what they apply. An empty array is the positive claim that a cast applies nothing; an absent field means unmeasured and falls back to the character's element at the cast, flagged provisional. It is the nominal schedule (gcsim emits damage for hits its own ICD suppressed). Normals carry no list (their hitmark is `frames.hit`, their element is owned by `resolveActionElement`), and neither do summons (`interval`/`firstTickDelay` say it). `units` is authored; absent means unmeasured, defaulting to 1U, provisional. A gcsim Durability-0 hit is an entry with no `element`: still a landing, on no counter, never deleted.
- **`nonHitting`** is the positive claim that an action lands no hit: no tint, no gauge offer, no hit record. On a normal ability it is `{normal|charged|plunge: true}`, read off the resolved normal ability (never in `resolveActionElement`, which the editor shares and which has no state). On a skill or burst it is `true`, and a boolean on a variant (Kujou Sara's C2 re-enables the hit), gated through `castLandsNoHit`, which reads the cast's `variantLabel`, never an input match. ⚠️ "Applies nothing" (`application: []`) is not this: such a cast still lands a hit. A summon's ticks are the series' hits, not the cast's. Records: `docs/rounds/lauma-charged-forms-round.md` §9, `docs/rounds/nonhitting-casts-round.md` §6.

## Abilities and effects

- **`Ability` / `AbilityVariant`**: a flat structure with `FrameMap` timing per hit. `ConstellationAbility.constellationLevel` is the canonical gate. ⚠️ A variant's `effects` and `summons` replace the base ability's, never merge, so a variant restating one effect drops the rest. `summons: []` on a variant means none, not "inherit".
- **`Effect`** is a discriminated union (buff, damage, heal, shield, resShred, energy, infuse, …). Heal, damage and shield carry an optional `flat`; a shield also carries `label`, its in-game name (never invent one). `Condition` trees gate effects, under the strict `condition-shape` lint. `physical` is an element only where damage is dealt or shredded (a `damage` or `resShred` effect, an `application[]` entry), never in a team-element gate such as `teamElementCount`.
- **`Trigger`**: before adding a member, check that an existing one doesn't already name the same instant. Two names for one instant is how `onStanceEnd` became an orphan.
- **`AbilityTag`**: `heal | shield | summon | taunt | coordinatedAttack | cleanse | grouping`, a lint-enforced vocabulary.
- **`summons[]`** means a thing left in the world (Oz, Guoba, Bennett's field). A shield is a state on the character and is authored as an `Effect`. An instant cast's active window is `activeDuration`, deliberately not `duration`: a dozen instant abilities carry a stray `duration` nothing reads.

## Summon ticks

- **`summonTick.tickCap` / `tickCapScaling`**: how many ticks a series fires when the count is not `duration ÷ interval`. Absent is right for most series, so it is not a field to fill in by default. It is a count, never a time bound: never nudge a stated `duration` to fit (Mona). The rule is `tickCapOf` in `sim/summons.ts`, and ⚠️ both readers must honour it (`collectSummonTicks` for the markers, `aura.ts`'s chain for the gauge; `run.ts` forbids unifying them). A cap widens the window by one interval so the boundary tick lands. With no `firstTickDelay`, a series first fires at the interval, not at 0. A cap and `durationExtensions` do not compose (Collei is uncapped for that reason). ⚠️ A sibling heal series on the same cadence must be capped too; the oracle can't see it, because a heal leaves no damage event. Lint D.27d. Record: `docs/rounds/gauge-round-L5e.md` §4.
- **A series that changes cadence** is authored as the same series twice with mutually exclusive `conditions`, not as an interval override, because the ICD group and the count move with the rate (Aino's burst under Moonsign, Ororon at C4; the class is closed). ⚠️ `summonTickSeries` drops only a definitive false, so a pair whose condition is unresolvable fires both: every reader of a tick series must take the same `ConditionContext`. The oracle cannot adjudicate either character.

## Buffs, procs and stacks

- **`BuffEffect.onFieldDelay`**: how long the receiver must have been on field before a buff lands (Nicole). Only on `target: 'active'` (lint B.14). Its waiver is a `Condition`, not a bespoke key: a Hexerei receiver skips the wait (`activeCharFaction`, never `teamFaction`), and at C6 everyone does. Only a definitive true waives. ⚠️ Don't split a pair that shares a `stat` into two `activeCharFaction` effects: the active-target pass dedupes by stat before per-segment evaluation and keeps whichever was authored first.
- **Proc triggers.** A `damage` effect with its own `application[]` on a trigger that listens to someone else's output (`onReaction`, `onHit`, `whileNormalAttacking`), armed by a cast's `armedFor`, a summon's liveness or a stack (`armedWhileStacks`), dispatched by one registry, `sim/procs.ts`. ⚠️ `icdGate` is not `procCooldown`: the cooldown says how often a proc fires, the gate how often a fire applies. Absent means standard. Pair the gate with the stream our effect models, never with the character (the Emilie rule). `whileNormalAttacking` is summon-hosted only (lint B.13c), and its markers come from `TimelineWindow.ticks`, never from `collectSummonTicks`. Record: `docs/rounds/coordinated-attacks-round.md`.
- **`gainStacks.scope`**: `'segment'` (the default) lives for the turn, so a same-character `>` continues the pool (`TurnCarry` in `sim/run.ts`); `'rotation'` survives swaps. A stack key names a resource, which either survives a swap or does not. Rotation-scoped keys live in `CharState.rotationStacks` and are mirrored into the live segment pool at every settle point: the scope decides the container, never the reader. It is a different axis from `trigger.source`; lint refuses `scope` on a team/others grant. Declare it on every grant of the key (lint D.39). Record: `docs/rounds/summon-tick-stacks-round.md` §10.
- **`onStack`**, a stack threshold: `{ keys, count, dominant?, label? }` fires when the sum of `keys` reaches `count`, and spends the set. A gate is a `stacks` Condition, not this. `sim/thresholds.ts` is the one reader, at the four settle points (reaction, cast, hit, tick). A key is read from its one home. A `damage` on it draws a mark on the accruing summon's lane and applies nothing (`application: []` is required); a `shield` opens at the fire; a `buff` anchors on the `stackThreshold` event. Lint B.16, B.13k, D.40; guarded by `sim/thresholds.test.ts`. Record: `docs/rounds/stack-threshold-round.md` §5.
- **Alhaitham's Mirrors** (record `docs/rounds/alhaitham-mirrors-round.md`): `gainStacks.lifetimeChain` makes stacks leave one at a time, `lifetime` apart, on a clock a grant into an empty pool starts and one past `max` restarts (lint B.13f3). `grantDelay` lands an `onCast` grant later, judged at the cast, into the turn's pool or nowhere (B.13f4). `onHit.armedWhileStacks` arms a proc for the rotation, fired only while the holder's own hit carries the key; `ElementalApplication.atStacks` picks its landings by count (B.13b). `ElementalApplication.minStacksAtCast` lands a cast's entry only at that count at the press (D.56). `BuffEffect.opensAfter` opens a cast's window later, if its holder is on field then (B.14b). `Ability.drawsStacks` draws a segment-scoped lifetime key as a stepped summon-lane row (D.57; `sim/stackLanes.ts`, record `docs/rounds/mirror-row-round.md`).
- **`gainStacks.accrual {group, window}`** is continuous accrual (`sim/accrual.ts`): `amount` becomes a rate per second, polled every 3 frames while a shared status is live, settled lazily and monotonically; the fire is predicted, scheduled and generation-stamped. `procCooldown` on an `onReaction` grant is honoured, but it is not how a rate is stated.

## States and stances

- **`terminatesOnAction` and `onEnd`** on a `TransientStateSpec`: a state can end on the owner's next attack or cast (N, C, P, E, Q; never a dash or jump, per Nate) and chain into another when it ends by any cause (`onEnd: {state, duration}`). A cooldown on something other than an E or Q is a named state that opens when another closes, never a `cooldown` number, because the sibling ability's `conditions` need a name to read. ⚠️ `settleOwnerStates` runs at the top of every attack and cast branch, before the state snapshot; `computeCurrentStates` stays a pure reader. A silent expiry's chain is materialised lazily; pending chains are an index on `CharState`, never a scan. Guarded by `sim/states.test.ts`.
- **Qualified states:** `stateKey()` is the only spelling of a state qualified by its source (`duringSummon:burst`); never hand-write `` `${state}:${source}` ``. `whileState` (a buff window intersected with the holder's `state` windows) is a different claim from a `state` condition, which is an entry test: it reads `state` windows only (lint D.45), and `consumeStacks.closesStateWhenEmpty` is onHit-only (lint D.46).
- **`summonsFollowState` + `TransientStateSpec.toggle`**: one window whose occupant is re-chosen mid-flight (Furina's Ousia and Pneuma). The flag is opt-in, because the general rule would be wrong: a burst variant whose stance ends mid-window keeps the summon it spawned. `toggle: true` names the non-default half (the state is `pneuma`; its absence is Ousia, so never mint an `ousia`). It re-resolves at state edges the parse can see, never at a timed window's expiry; lint requires the gating state to be a toggle. `CastEvent.variantLabel` has three states: a string (that variant), `null` (the base), `undefined` (match by input).
- **`cooldownStartsAtStanceEnd`** (Xilonen, Ifa) starts the clock at the stance's end. Never encode it as `cooldownStartDelay` equal to the duration: that field means animation frames (lint D.21/D.22). Ifa's `terminatesOnSwap` stays `false`, and that is correct: he must land before he can swap. Record: `docs/rounds/stance-cooldown-round.md`.

## Elements and cooldowns

- **`ElementRef`**: a field naming an element takes a literal or `{ type: 'teamElementPriority', order, fallback? }`, resolved at parse time off `teamElementCounts`. With no context it resolves to nothing, deliberately unlike the Conditions' optimistic `null`.
- **`NormalAbility.coInfusion`** (Varka) is offered after `infusion`, and ⚠️ the order is the mechanic: conversion-then-Anemo swirls, Anemo-then-conversion does nothing. It decides the input block's tint (Nate), and lands no second hit record or action event. Two counters, `${char}:${tag}:co`. `sE` is a stance's special skill (it is in `CAST_TOKEN_RE`); `sC` reads `specialChargeFrames`, falling back to `chargeFrames`. Record: `docs/curation/varka-two-elements-scope.md` §7.
- **`cooldownReduce`** (`sim/cooldownReduce.ts`): ⚠️ a cooldown lives in two homes (the charge queue that places the next cast, and the cast event's `readyAt` that draws the ghost tail), and `reduceCooldown` moves both. Charges recharge one at a time, chained. A `reduces: 'skill'` moves the skill active at the fire, except an effect hosted on a skill, which reduces its host (`hostSkillId`). `procCap { count, per: 'stance' }` (lint B.17); `startsOnCooldown` (lint D.44). A reduction that is a static team fact at the cast is a `cooldownModifier`, not this. Never author a coin flip (Nate: a low-probability proc is never drawn at 100% uptime). The token blocks are the reduction's whole surface: no bar and no printed amount (Nate). Anemo Resonance applies over the roster without the full-party rule (Nate: the notation names who acts), percentages before flats. Guarded by `sim/cooldownReduce.test.ts`. Record: `docs/rounds/cooldown-reduction-round.md` §4.

# Notes — `vz_breakdown` (Damage breakdown)

Hand-written half of the ledger for `../inventory/vz_breakdown.md`. Written at the close of its
stage round (2026-11-03, `docs/rounds/viz-stage-breakdown-2026-11-03.md`), where Nate chose the

## The surface

One stage box: a headline, the four families as view tabs, a setup bar (Reaction, Stats, Your
level, Enemy), the formula as upright columns (rows on a narrow tile) with a cap per roll, each
column's foot, and fine print. No Readout card.

- ⚓ **The headline is the best next roll** (Nate): "+2.3% damage from one more CRIT Rate roll".
  Rolls that tie at the figure shown are all named: "{A} or {B}", "{A}, {B}, or {C}" (the
  Oxford comma). ⚠️ ASSEMBLED — the figure leads in English, the stat names are a LOOKUP, and
  the list joins are English; a locale needs the whole sentence per count, ⛔ not a joined list.
- **The headline's nouns:** "damage" on a direct hit, "{Reaction} damage" on the reaction
  families (an additive reaction's gain is on the damage it adds, not the whole hit), "Stellar
  Swirl blast damage" for both blasts. The reaction names are 🎮 game terms.
- **The Stats segment's face** is the sheet at a glance in the artifact card's short names: "ATK
  2,440 · DMG 61.6% · CR 70% · CD 140%" (+ "· EM 205" with a reaction). 🏷 CR, CD, EM, ATK%
  are the community's and the game card's abbreviations; keep them. The Enemy face: "Lv 100 ·
  RES 10%", "· DEF −20%" once a shred or an ignore is set (a true minus sign).
- **The chart's words** are furniture: the y axis "times a hit from base ATK alone" (direct) or
  "damage"; a cap's label is its short name over "+2.3%"; each column's foot is its name, the
  sheet's numbers, and its multiplier with its change.
- **"The marks at the end of a bar"** names the caps in prose (the fine print, Help). ⚓ "End",
  not "top", because the narrow form's bars lie flat. ⛔ Not "caps": that is the code's word.
- **The fine print** at rest is the family's caption, then the marks' sentence, then the hint
  ("Hover or tap a column to read it, and drag one with a grip to change its stat." upright;
  "Hover or tap a row to read it." in rows). Direct hit's caption opens "Each bar multiplies the
  hit … the same size wherever it sits and a cut runs the other way" so it holds for both forms.
- **A column's reading** is its sentence, its share of the boost, its rolls ("One more CRIT DMG
  roll (+6.6%) would raise the damage by 2.3%."), then its formula. Readings take "would"
  (style guide §1.8).
- **Retired on the stage:** "What the next roll buys", the table's "Substat", "One roll" and
  "Damage gained", "Each row adds one average 5★ roll of that substat to your current sheet.",
  the Build and Enemy kickers, "DEF shred and ignore" with its On and Off, and the reaction row's
  "picked above".
- 🔒 **Frozen (game vocabulary):** ATK · HP · DEF · RES · EM · CRIT Rate · CRIT DMG · DMG Bonus ·
  Elemental Mastery · Base DMG Bonus · Elevation · the reaction names (🎮). The formulas in the
  readings embed English words (`yours`, `theirs`, `shred`, `ignore`, `RES`); ⛔ a translator
  must not localize them.

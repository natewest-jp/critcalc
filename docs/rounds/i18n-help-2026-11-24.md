# Help — 2026-11-24

Round 8 of the translation item in `docs/todo.md`, after the tool interiors
(`i18n-interiors-2026-11-23.md`): Help's four chapters (the FAQ, Team builder, Rotation
planner, and Visualizers) and the area shell, extracted with English unchanged and drafted in
Japanese from `docs/text/terms-ja.md`, still hidden. Stacked branches: `feat/i18n-help`
(extraction), `feat/i18n-ja-help` (the Japanese draft), and this record on `docs/i18n-help`.

## What was built

- **235 messages extracted** into `messages/en.json` (2,407 in all), under `faq`, `help`,
  `help_planner`, `help_tb`, and `help_vz`.
- **A paragraph is one message.** Its bold words, code, links, and screenshot numbers are
  markup, so a translation can move each one: `{#ref}2{/ref}` renders `HelpRef` 2, and each
  link is its own tag (`{#coverage}`, `{#feedback}`). Each chapter says once, in a `p`
  snippet over `Msg.svelte`, what its tags render as. The composer's two underline colors are
  their own tags (`warn`, `approx`), so they keep their classes.
- **Folded fragments:** a `HelpRef` inside a paragraph split it into several ledger entries.
  Each split paragraph is now one message, keyed by the slug of the whole text, as the
  extractor would mint it.
- **Literal braces** are escaped in the message (`Blank E\{8s\}`), and the formulas keep their
  non-breaking spaces. `messages.test.ts` now reads an escaped brace as text.
- **Chapter titles are getters** in `areas.ts`, and so are the menu's chapter labels in
  `menuState.svelte.ts`, which copied them at module load. The About area's titles (What's
  new, Contribute, Beta, Privacy, Coverage) are messages now, so the menu names them in
  Japanese, though their pages are still English.
- **The Japanese draft** covers every new message. Section G of `terms-ja.md` lists the ten
  words the prose added, and open question 14 is new.

## English changes

None. The prerendered `/help`, `/help/planner`, `/help/builder`, and `/help/visualizers` (and
About, What's new, Privacy, and Coverage) match `main`'s build tag by tag and text node by text
node, spaces and `&nbsp;` included.

## Choices in the Japanese draft

- Character and ability names in the prose are the game's Japanese, read from genshin-db's
  talent text (夢見月瑞希の夢浮かみ, 冥蝶の舞, ファジオ). Notation stays as written
  (`Mizuki E ~2s`).
- The composer takes the English game's display names, so the Planner chapter says
  英語版ゲームの表示名 where English says "the game's display names".
- Banner wording quoted in the prose follows the live UI's Japanese (チーム2に追加中 · 3/4,
  コロンビーナを入れ替え中), with the character's Japanese name, which the live banner will show
  once the game-data lookups exist.

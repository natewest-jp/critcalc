# Visualizers overhaul, round 3: the rail (2026-11-02)

**Status:** built on `feat/viz-rail` (stacked on round 2's `feat/viz-type-kit`), not merged.
⏭ Nate's look. Design: `docs/design/visualizers-design.md` §3, §4 and §6.

## 1. Why

Nate's questions: which controls belong highest, which mustn't move while you use them, and how
far the set-once folding of Wish odds should go. Measured before: the chart took the height
the controls left (`minmax(min-content, 1fr)`), so a control that grew its panel (a note that
changed length, Custom in the ICD rule, the DEF toggle) shrank the chart and lifted the panel
under the pointer. One up, the controls sat under a chart up to 650px wide while the Readout
ran the tile's height as a slab of mostly empty paper.

## 2. The shared form (`app.css` § THE RAIL)

- **Four slots:** chart, live, settings, Readout. `.instrument-controls` is now an alias of the
  settings slot.
- **The Readout is last at every width** (narrow: chart, live, settings, Readout; mid: chart
  across, live over settings on the left, the Readout on the right with the last row
  absorbing its extra height; wide: the rail on the right). It is the slot whose height follows
  the values, so a growing answer moves nothing under the hand. Measured: Wish odds' Goal line
  stays at 363px when choosing C6 grows the Readout by seven rows.
- **The chart's height is the tile's:** at the wide tier `100cqh` and `position: sticky`, so a
  rail taller than the tile scrolls past a chart that holds still; at the mid tier 🎛️
  `--instrument-chart-mid` (0.55) of the tile; `min-height: max-content` keeps each chart's
  natural floor. Only from 1024px, where the tile is a size container.
- **A control's dependents sit below it.** Live normally comes first, because settings usually
  change only the answer. `.instrument--settings-first` reverses it where the settings decide
  what the live panel offers (Artifact odds: a second stat adds a row to The piece, which used to
  push the stat chip just tapped out from under the finger).
- **`kit/SettingLine.svelte`:** a folded set-once line (label, the value at the label's size, the
  setting in a 🎛️ 22rem popover). Wish odds' Goal and Radiance counter moved onto it.
- The Readout no longer runs the full height at the wide tier (⚓ 2026-10-06 had it reach the
  bottom of its column); it is natural height, last in the rail. For Nate's eye.

## 3. Per instrument

| instrument       | live                                                                    | settings                                                  |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wish odds        | Pulls saved                                                             | per banner: pity, guarantee, Goal (line), Radiance (line) |
| Artifact odds    | the view's section (Farming, The piece, Your piece + Reshape)           | Piece, Your stats, **first** (settings-first)             |
| Reaction curves  | Elemental Mastery                                                       | Build: Reaction Bonus, Level (line)                       |
| Enemy RES & DEF  | Shred (heads the right column, scrolls)                                 | Your hit (Your level as a line), Enemy                    |
| ICD timeline     | Attack string                                                           | ICD rule                                                  |
| Gauge explorer   | Example scenarios (the chart's toolbar and pills are the live controls) | —                                                         |
| Damage breakdown | Build (Level as a line), under the waterfall                            | Enemy, under Build                                        |
| Crit heatmap     | CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, then Hits                                          | the overlays, last in the strip                           |

Details and judgment calls:

- **Enemy RES & DEF** keeps its bespoke grid. At the wide tier the right column is the tile's
  height: the shred list first, filling it and scrolling inside, then the Readout at its foot,
  so a Readout that grows shortens the list's window rather than moving its rows. 🎛️ The list
  keeps at least 10rem. At mid the charts take 55% of the tile like the rest.
- **ICD timeline:** the legend items that can appear mid-scrub ("applied by the counter",
  "window limit reached") render whenever the rule allows them and hide with `visibility`, and
  sit at the legend's end. One up, the one-row strip centres with a lot of bare night above and
  below (`STALK_GROW` ⚓ Nate's); for Nate's eye.
- **Gauge explorer:** the scenarios stay in the open (they are how a player learns the
  reactions) and take the live slot, so no empty row draws a gap above the rail. The aura log
  is no longer clipped at 21rem on a phone (bounded by the 8-application cap).
- **Damage breakdown:** the waterfall wants width, so its controls stay under it and its rail is
  the Readout alone (its own areas; the chart opts out of the tile-height rule). The long DEF
  formula now follows the switch, not the first step of a drag, so the row stops jumping
  mid-drag; at 0 with the switch on it reads "−0% · −0%". Value cells hold `6ch`. Still able to
  move mid-drag: the label column (a detail gaining characters, "EM + Reaction Bonus" when
  Reaction Bonus leaves 0).
- **Crit heatmap:** Pin this build / Clear pin is one button that keeps its place (both labels
  stacked in one cell), the pinned rows below it; the sentence over Roll and the "No-crit risk
  (n hits)" label reserve their longest forms. Open for Nate: at 40–60rem the distribution
  section is bottom-aligned (⚓ Nate), so a first roll of 30+ hits wraps the dots and lifts Roll
  10–30px.

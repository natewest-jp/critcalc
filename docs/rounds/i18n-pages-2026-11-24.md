# The pages — 2026-11-24

Round 9 of the translation item in `docs/todo.md`, after Help (`i18n-help-2026-11-24.md`):
the About area's pages (About, What's new, Contribute, Beta, and Privacy), extracted with
English unchanged and drafted in Japanese from `docs/text/terms-ja.md`, still hidden. Stacked
branches: `feat/i18n-pages` (extraction), `feat/i18n-ja-pages` (the Japanese draft), and this
record on `docs/i18n-pages`. With them, every page of the site has a Japanese draft.

## What was built

- **118 messages extracted** into `messages/en.json` (2,525 in all), under `about`,
  `changelog`, `contribute`, `beta`, and `privacy`, as Help did it: a paragraph is one
  message, its bold words, code, and links are markup, and each chapter says once, in a `p`
  snippet over `Msg.svelte`, what its tags render as.
- **Dates are not messages.** What's new writes each entry's date and each month heading from
  the ISO day in the page's language, and Privacy's "Last updated {date}." takes the date as a
  placeholder (`LAST_UPDATED` in `Privacy.svelte`). The formatter is `formatDay` in
  `src/lib/i18n/dates.ts`, read and written in UTC so the build machine's time zone can't move
  a day, tested beside it. The ledger's changelog surface dropped from 61 messages to 47.
- **A new What's new entry** now adds its title and paragraphs as `changelog_*` messages; the
  "To add an entry" comment in `Changelog.svelte` says so. Each one needs a Japanese line too
  before Japanese opens, or it shows English there.
- **The Japanese draft** covers every new message. Section H of `terms-ja.md` lists the words
  the pages added, and open questions 15 and 16 are new.

## English changes

None. The prerendered `/about`, `/changelog`, `/contribute`, `/beta`, and `/privacy` match
`main`'s build tag by tag and text node by text node once whitespace collapses as the browser
collapses it (the old source wrapped lines inside paragraphs). Non-breaking spaces were
compared as written.

## Choices in the Japanese draft

- Ability names in What's new are the game's Japanese, from genshin-db's talent text
  (重撃·衡平な裁量, 変転・漆黒の否, 翦月輪, 化海月, 琢光鏡, 万象、灰に帰す). Constellations are
  C1 to C6, as entry 88 rules, never 凸.
- **Witch's Revelation** is not in genshin-db yet, so the draft has 魔女の啓示, unchecked (open
  question 15).
- **The wish counter** follows the existing draft's 輝映カウンター, though 輝映 is the Stellar
  reactions' word and the mechanic is 掴みし明光 (open question 16). The What's new entry uses
  掴みし明光 for the mechanic and 輝映カウンター for the control.
- The first person in Beta and Contribute is です・ます with 私 where English says "I". "Calling
  all mains!" is キャラ愛好家の皆さん、出番です！
- The migrated profile's name stays English in quotes (「My profile」), as the notices have it.

# Vodyanitsa's 5.2.15 text revision (2026-11-11)

Branch `data/vodyanitsa-5-2-15`. `audit-genshin-db-text.mjs --since 5.2.14` named two
revised entries: her Elemental Skill (Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn) and her C2 (Echoes
That Pierce the Snow), both with no mechanic delta.

## 1. What changed in the text

A field-by-field diff of genshin-db 5.2.14 against 5.2.15 found wording only:

- Skill: "the party's active character gains increased resistance to interruption and
  periodically regenerates HP" became "your active character gains …".
- C2: "dealt by the currently active party member" and "caused by the currently
  active party member" both became "your own currently active party member".

The skill's name, its nine scaling rows and every parameter are unchanged, so the
talent sidecar (`docs/curation/talents/vodyanitsa.json`) stays as it is. The only
other change in her entry is the character profile's `region`, which 5.2.15 now
fills with "Snezhnaya", the value we already carry.

The new wording scopes the heal and the CRIT DMG buff to the player's own active
character in co-op. Single-player behaviour is the same.

## 2. Cross-checks

## 3. What was authored

Both `description` strings now carry the 5.2.15 text verbatim. No effects, frames or
caveats: she stays a frameless stub in `FRAMELESS_EXEMPT`, and the revision changes
no mechanic, so no test was added. Her effects (the skill's Hydro/Cryo RES shred and
heal, the C2 CRIT DMG buff and so on) remain unauthored, which is the stub-upgrade
round's job.

# The Simplified Chinese draft — 2026-11-25

The second locale, after Japanese (`docs/design/i18n-plan.md` §6 has what Japanese taught).
Everything Japanese built carried over, so Chinese was the term list, the game's names, and the
draft, in one session on stacked branches:

1. `i18n/zh-terms`: `docs/text/terms-zh.md`, and ⚓ Nate's ruling, Simplified only for now.
2. `i18n/zh-data`: the game's Chinese names for abilities and Travelers.
3. `i18n/zh-draft`: all of `messages/zh.json` and `src/lib/data/kitNotes/zh.json`.
4. `i18n/zh-close-out`: Help's screenshots in Chinese.
5. `fix/ja-game-terms`: four Japanese words the Chinese lookups settled.
6. `docs/i18n-zh-record`: this record and the to-do.

`/zh` stays hidden and `noindex`, like `/ja`, until Chinese players have reviewed it.

## What was built

- **The term list** keeps the Japanese list's numbers, so entry 31 is the same concept in both.
  Chinese is the game's original language, so every game word comes from the game's Chinese
  text map, matched to the English line by hash. The players' words come from the bilibili
  wiki's element, application, and damage-bracket pages (元素量, 附着元素, 触发元素, 计次器,
  乘区, 减抗, 剧变反应) and community guides (双暴分, 软保底, 歪, 国家队, 双水). The wiki's slang
  page refuses automated reads, so words Claude couldn't confirm are marked unsourced, and the
  real splits are eight open questions. Section L lists the twenty words the draft settled.
- **Ability names:** `generate-ability-names.mjs` reads Chinese through the same four sources
  as Japanese (per-locale label suffixes, 的 for の, and the game's · as the middle dot), with
  36 hand matches, each citing its game line. 0 split, 0 missing.
- **Travelers:** genshin-db's elements carry no Chinese, and `generate-game-names.mjs` runs on
  every build, where the text maps are absent, so the seven one-character element names are a
  cited table there: 旅行者（风）.
- **The endgame** needed nothing: lunaris serves Chinese, and `inLang` already reads it.
- **The draft,** 2,608 messages and 338 kit notes, was drafted in six parts by area plus the
  notes, in parallel. A validator checked each part (every key, the same placeholders and tags,
  plurals with only the `other` case, no CD, no kana). Then a sweep over the merged file
  aligned the labels Help quotes in its prose with the labels themselves, and one word per
  concept across parts (更新日志 for What's new).
- **Checks:** Chinese joins `COMPLETE` in `messages.test.ts`, `kitNotes.test.ts` covers its
  notes, `abilityNames.test.ts` its names, and `AVOID` rules out CD (entry 28).
- **Help's screenshots:** 21 in Chinese; four match English. Checked by eye: the Inspector,
  the phone dock, and Wish odds.

## Bugs the draft found, fixed

- `generate-ability-names.mjs` gave Zibai's Spirit Steed's Stride as 灵驹飞踏第: the label
  第一段伤害 matched the suffix 一段伤害. The longer suffix is read first now.
- Artifact odds showed the unit "CV" from a literal; it is `vz_artifact_cv_unit` now (双暴分).
- Wish odds measured its chart labels by `.length`, half a CJK label's width; it counts
  columns now. Latin text measures the same as before.

## English unchanged

`en.json` differs from `main` only by `vz_artifact_cv_unit`, whose text is the old literal.
The prerendered `/zh` pages show no Latin word that `/ja` doesn't, beyond the players' own
"bug" and a bracketed User-Agent. The sample rotations keep English names on purpose, as in
Japanese.

## Japanese, from the same lookups

Reading the Japanese text map line for line beside the Chinese answered parts of the Japanese
community request: Witch's Revelation is 魔女の諭示 (open question 15), Menacing is マスター
(14), the Theater's heading is 主な相手 (half of 6), and Epitomized Path is 神鋳軌定, not
神鋳定軌. The draft and the Wish odds shot follow. Protective Canopy has a game name,
交錯の護り, but the draft names the other resonances the players' way, so question 12 stays
open, reworded. The answered questions stay numbered, since the HoYoLAB post maps onto them.

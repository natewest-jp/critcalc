**Core thesis:** index by _question_, not by character. Wikis are character-indexed, tier lists verdict-indexed; nothing is question-indexed ("who can heal my team off-field at the constellations I own?"). Capabilities are **derived from curated effects with provenance** (badge → the ability + condition granting it), never hand-maintained labels. Pitch: _the Rotation Planner answers "when"; the Team Builder answers "who."_

**Three layers, independently shippable:**

- **L1 Find (MVP):** roster grid; identity facets (element/weapon/rarity/region) + capability facets derived from effects (heal/shield/resShred/buff-stat-X/off-field/taunt/NB/Moonsign); three-way constellation filter mode "at C0 / at MY constellations / at max C" via the existing site-wide `constellations` store; **coverage audit script is a first-class deliverable** (tags-vs-derivable-effects mismatches → the curation backlog).
- **L2 Compare:** 2–4 cards aligned BY ROW (CDs on one line, Q costs on one line); **uptime glyphs** — tiny static bars reusing the planner's three-phase bar language at a shared time scale.
- **L3 Assemble:** 4 team slots; derived team FACTS (resonance, faction bonuses, NB, Moonsign) + coverage _checklist_ (✓/✗ facts, never a synergy score); cross-injection via `?team=` URLs both directions — including planner "replace this slot" → Team Builder with filters pre-seeded from the outgoing character's capabilities.

   **exactly 1.500 s** after the previous application and did _not_ apply — the

3. **The spawn spacing is not fixed.** Gaps between first hits: 0.30 s then 1.59 s

   (~0.97 s apart), matching neither run.
4. **Y2 throws four radishes in both runs** (gcsim: three). Its spawn came earlier
   than gcsim's 162 f, which is enough to fit a fourth throw before gcsim's 360 f
   removal; nothing here says the burst outlasts 6 s.
5. **Y1 matches our single modelled Yuegui in count** (four), landing 0.15–0.24 s
   later than our cast + 2.43 / 3.43 / 4.43 / 5.43 s. Our two gauge applications
   (2.43, 4.43) sit within ~0.25 s of the game's (run 2: 2.669, 4.619) and match in
   number. With the anchor uncertain by the dummy timer's offset, no data change.

## Dahlia — Sacramental Shower lifetime, untouched (Hold E)

**Setup.** No log line exists for this (the Shower deals no damage unless an
opponent touches it), so Nate timed it by eye: Hold E aimed **away** from the
dummy, then watched the Shower until it faded. Several tries.

**Finding.** It faded **about 10.5 s after the E press, every time**, which is
about **10 s after the Shower appears** (the Hold release spawns it at ~28 f ≈
0.47 s, gcsim `skillHitmarks`). Stopwatch reads have run ~0.4 s off the log
before (2026-10-14), so read this as 10 s ± about half a second.

⚠️ This is the **untouched** lifetime. An opponent touching the Shower ends it at
once with the Hydro explosion, which is what gcsim (and our `application[]` at
f27) model.
